# Supplementary figure caption: survey map and timing

Geographic coverage and timing of the 124 DHS and MIS surveys and 47 MICS surveys with complete birth histories (41 countries). Circles are DHS and MIS surveys and triangles MICS surveys; filled symbols are the 99 DHS and 37 MICS surveys that contribute to the primary analysis (37 countries), and open symbols the 35 excluded surveys: 10 in Lesotho (5; malaria free, not processed), the Comoros (2) and São Tomé and Príncipe (3), which have no Snow et al. malaria-endemic polygon, and 25 for which a required covariate was unavailable for every survey × unit area after the declared substitutions (14 DHS, all 5 MIS and 6 MICS surveys; for each, the first missing covariate is listed in the primary's complete_case_losses.csv). Country shading indicates the number of included surveys; grey countries have none. Timeline rows are grouped by UN sub-region (Central Africa: UN Middle Africa), and n is the number of mothers (complete birth histories) contributing at least one child to the primary analysis, summed over the country's included surveys (1,126,610 in total). Where a MICS survey shares a country and year with a DHS or MIS survey (SEN 2015), the MICS symbol is drawn slightly above the line. The 46 MICS surveys without a complete birth history are not shown.

Country outlines are dissolved from the most recent available boundary file in each country (DHS files, or the analysis-region polygons built for MICS). Survey year describes fieldwork, not the calendar year assigned to each child's band entry. Per-survey exclusion reasons are in survey_timeline_all.csv.
