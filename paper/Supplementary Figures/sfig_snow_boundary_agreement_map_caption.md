# Survey-regions against the Snow et al. polygons

Agreement between the survey-regions used in the analysis and the Snow et al. (2017) polygons, for the most recent survey of the Snow primary sample in each of 38 countries (447 survey-regions; DHS boundary files and MICS analysis regions). Survey-regions are coloured by how they relate to the Snow polygons, using GPW 2020 population. Same area: at least 90% of the region's population lies in one Snow polygon, and at least 90% of that polygon's population lies in the region. Region inside a larger Snow polygon: at least 90% of the region lies in one polygon that also covers other regions. Snow polygons nested in the region: the region spans several polygons, each lying (at least 90%) within it. Boundaries cross: the region spans several polygons and at least one of them extends well beyond it. White lines are survey-region boundaries and thin dark lines Snow polygon boundaries; country borders are black. Malaria-free and island units are the 31 Snow units outside the 520; the shapefile has no polygon for the Comoros, Zanzibar or São Tomé and Príncipe. Countries without a survey in the sample are light grey; white areas are regions of a mapped survey that are not in the sample (CM71FL centre, CM71FL littoral, MC_SWZ2021 manzini, MR71FL dakhletnouadhibou, NM61FL hardap, NM61FL karas, ZA71FL easterncape, ZA71FL northerncape, ZA71FL westerncape). Mapped survey-regions: same area 206 (38% of population); region inside a larger Snow polygon 139 (18% of population); Snow polygons nested in the region 56 (37% of population); boundaries cross 37 (7% of population); malaria-free or island unit 5 (1% of population); no Snow polygon 4. Across the 1261 survey-regions of all 138 sample surveys: same area 632 (46% of child-band records); region inside a larger Snow polygon 284 (17% of child-band records); Snow polygons nested in the region 236 (30% of child-band records); boundaries cross 83 (7% of child-band records); malaria-free or island unit 16; no Snow polygon 10. Mapped surveys: AGO DHS 2023, BDI DHS 2016, BEN MICS 2021, BFA DHS 2021, CAF MICS 2018, CIV DHS 2021, CMR DHS 2018, COD DHS 2023, COG MICS 2014, COM MICS 2022, ETH DHS 2024, GAB DHS 2019, GHA DHS 2022, GIN DHS 2018, GMB DHS 2019, GNB MICS 2018, KEN DHS 2022, LBR DHS 2019, MDG DHS 2021, MLI DHS 2023, MOZ DHS 2022, MRT DHS 2020, MWI DHS 2024, NAM DHS 2013, NER DHS 2012, NGA DHS 2024, RWA DHS 2019, SEN DHS 2023, SLE DHS 2019, SSD MICS 2010, SWZ MICS 2021, TCD MICS 2019, TGO MICS 2017, TZA DHS 2022, UGA DHS 2016, ZAF DHS 2016, ZMB DHS 2024, ZWE MICS 2019.
