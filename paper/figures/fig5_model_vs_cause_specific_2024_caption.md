# Figure 5 caption — PfPR-ACM against cause-specific malaria mortality, by country

**Figure. Malaria-attributable under-five mortality in 2024: PfPR-ACM model against cause-specific estimates.** Each point is a country; rates are deaths per 1,000 child-years under 5. (A) PfPR-ACM estimates using UN IGME all-cause mortality (y) against UN IGME (CA-CODE) malaria deaths (x), both over the UN WPP population under 5. (B) PfPR-ACM estimates using IHME all-cause mortality (y) against IHME malaria deaths (x), both over IHME person-years. The PfPR-ACM estimate counts all deaths averted if prevalence fell to zero (direct and indirect); the cause-specific estimates count deaths assigned to malaria as the underlying cause. The dashed line is equality; countries are coloured by UN M49 sub-region (Middle Africa as Central Africa), and those at 2.5 or more per 1,000 on either axis are labelled with their ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 codes. Spearman correlation: 0.91 (A) and 0.91 (B); the PfPR-ACM estimate is higher in 37 (A) and 34 (B) of 42 countries. CA-CODE takes malaria from WHO estimates in 13 low-burden countries; South Sudan's IHME all-cause mortality is its crisis-free value. Point estimates only.
