# Supplementary figure caption: polygons of the regional sub-analyses

GADM 4.1 admin-1 polygons in each regional sub-analysis of the subgroup refits, in the style of Figure 1. West, Central and East Africa hold the surveys of the UN M49 sub-regions of Figure 1 (Middle Africa as Central Africa). A polygon is coloured when its records are in the sub-analysis; mid grey marks the other polygons of the primary analysis. (A) West Africa: 173 polygons; 3,743,776 child-band records and 56,426 deaths from 61 surveys in 15 countries. (B) Central Africa: 113 polygons; 1,224,689 child-band records and 17,579 deaths from 18 surveys in 7 countries. (C) East Africa: 173 polygons; 2,735,874 child-band records and 33,671 deaths from 50 surveys in 12 countries. Southern Africa is not refitted (too few deaths and too little prevalence), so it has no panel.
