# Supplementary figure caption: pfpr_splines_by_subregion

Sensitivity of the fitted PfPR–mortality relationship to the analysis subset. Log mortality hazard ratios relative to PfPR[2–10] = 20% by completed-month age band, from the PfPR-ACM model fitted to the full primary sample (black, with its pointwise 95% conditional interval shaded) and refitted separately in three subsets: UN M49 Western Africa countries; UN M49 Middle (Central) Africa countries; and UN M49 Eastern Africa countries. Southern Africa (UN M49) is not refitted: it has 1,071 deaths in 7 surveys from 3 countries and PfPR[2–10] never exceeds 11.0% (95% of records below 7.4%), so its curve at 20% and 40% would be an extrapolation. Each subset refit uses the same seven separate age-band models, 17 standardised covariates with the full-sample scaling, fixed HIV incidence imputation, gamma = 2 and basis dimensions as the primary analysis, with knots placed at quantiles of the subset's own predictor values. Each curve is drawn over the central 95% of its own exposure distribution. Subset sizes. West Africa: 1,128,959 children, 3,743,776 records, 56,426 deaths, 766 survey × unit areas, 61 surveys, 15 countries; Central Africa: 366,299 children, 1,230,434 records, 17,628 deaths, 259 survey × unit areas, 18 surveys, 7 countries; East Africa: 824,178 children, 2,735,874 records, 33,671 deaths, 399 survey × unit areas, 50 surveys, 12 countries. The subsets overlap the full sample and one another, so the curves are not independent and their differences are descriptive; the comparison does not constitute a test of effect modification. Subset intervals are omitted for legibility and are available in the saved curve table.
