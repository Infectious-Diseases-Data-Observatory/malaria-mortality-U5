# Supplementary figure caption: pfpr_splines_by_period

Sensitivity of the fitted PfPR–mortality relationship to the analysis subset. Log mortality hazard ratios relative to PfPR[2–10] = 20% by completed-month age band, from the PfPR-ACM model fitted to the full primary sample (black, with its pointwise 95% conditional interval shaded) and refitted separately in four subsets: surveys conducted up to the median survey year (2014); surveys conducted after it; child-band records with calendar year up to the median calendar year of the records (2012.8); and records after it. Each subset refit uses the same seven separate age-band models, 17 standardised covariates with the full-sample scaling, fixed HIV incidence imputation, gamma = 2 and basis dimensions as the primary analysis, with knots placed at quantiles of the subset's own predictor values. Each curve is drawn over the central 95% of its own exposure distribution. Subset sizes. Surveys up to median survey year: 1,128,233 children, 3,567,241 records, 58,456 deaths, 720 survey × unit areas, 74 surveys, 34 countries; Surveys after median survey year: 1,207,635 children, 4,072,202 records, 46,658 deaths, 732 survey × unit areas, 62 surveys, 34 countries; Records up to median calendar year: 1,296,272 children, 3,858,211 records, 60,903 deaths, 956 survey × unit areas, 97 surveys, 36 countries; Records after median calendar year: 1,258,786 children, 3,781,232 records, 44,211 deaths, 963 survey × unit areas, 85 surveys, 35 countries. The subsets overlap the full sample and one another, so the curves are not independent and their differences are descriptive; the comparison does not constitute a test of effect modification. Subset intervals are omitted for legibility and are available in the saved curve table.
