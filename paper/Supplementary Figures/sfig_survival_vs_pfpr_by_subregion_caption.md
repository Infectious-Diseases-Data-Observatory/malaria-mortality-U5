# Supplementary figure caption: survival against PfPR by sub-region

Survival through each age band against PfPR[2–10] in the primary analysis sample, by UN sub-region. Each point is one survey × area unit × band-entry year: the proportion of children entering the band who survived it, at the unit's MAP PfPR[2–10] in that year, drawn as an open circle whose area is proportional to the number of children entering the band (the largest 1% of points are drawn at the maximum size; 52,749 points over the seven bands). Lines are unadjusted spline fits for each sub-region (binomial generalised additive model of deaths with a complementary log-log link and a penalised cubic regression spline of PfPR[2–10], basis dimension 5, gamma = 2, no covariates), drawn over the central 95% of the sub-region's exposure in each band, with pointwise 95% intervals shaded. Sub-regions (UN M49): Western Africa (61 surveys in 15 countries), Central Africa (18 surveys in 7 countries), Eastern Africa (50 surveys in 12 countries), Southern Africa (7 surveys in 3 countries); Central Africa is UN Middle Africa. Southern Africa is shown as points only: its PfPR[2–10] spans 0–10%, too narrow a range for a spline fit (it is not refitted in the sub-region sensitivity analysis either). All panels share the same y-axis; 748 points (0.6% of children) lie below 93.0% and are drawn at the lower edge as open triangles. The fits are descriptive: unlike the adjusted age-band models of the sub-region refits, they do not adjust for confounders, calendar time or survey and area effects, and they mix variation within and between countries.
