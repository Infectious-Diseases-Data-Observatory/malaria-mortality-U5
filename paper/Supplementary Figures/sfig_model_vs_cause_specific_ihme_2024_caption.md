# Supplementary figure caption — PfPR-ACM against IHME malaria mortality, by country

**Figure. Malaria-attributable under-five mortality in 2024: PfPR-ACM model with IHME all-cause mortality against IHME malaria deaths.** Each point is a country; rates are deaths per 1,000 child-years under 5. PfPR-ACM estimates using IHME all-cause mortality (y) against IHME malaria deaths (x), both over IHME person-years. The PfPR-ACM estimate counts all deaths averted if prevalence fell to zero (direct and indirect); IHME counts deaths assigned to malaria as the underlying cause. The dashed line is equality. Countries with an estimate of at least 1 per 1,000 from either source are named (29 of 42). Spearman correlation: 0.91; the PfPR-ACM estimate is higher in 30 of 42 countries. Vertical lines are 95% uncertainty intervals of the PfPR-ACM estimates, from 500 joint draws of MAP's national PfPR[2–10], the all-cause deaths and the PfPR–death curves (Supplementary Methods); horizontal lines are IHME's 95% uncertainty intervals. The lower bound exceeds the IHME estimate in 17 countries, and the upper bound is below it in 5. The axes are those of the main-text figure (UN IGME). South Sudan's IHME all-cause mortality is its crisis-free value.
