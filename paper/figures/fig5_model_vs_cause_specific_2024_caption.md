# Figure 5 caption — PfPR-ACM by age group against UN IGME malaria mortality, by country

**Figure. Malaria-attributable under-five mortality in 2024 by age group: PfPR-ACM model against UN IGME malaria deaths.** Each point is a country. The y-axis is the PfPR-ACM estimate of malaria-attributable deaths at (A) under 1 month, (B) 1–11 months and (C) 1–4 years, the age groups in which UN IGME reports all-cause deaths. The x-axis is the same in every panel: UN IGME (CA-CODE) malaria deaths under 5, which CA-CODE assigns to ages 1–59 months as a whole (none under 1 month). All rates are deaths per 1,000 child-years under 5 (UN WPP population under 5), so the three PfPR-ACM rates of a country sum to its under-five rate. The PfPR-ACM estimate counts all deaths averted if prevalence fell to zero (direct and indirect); UN IGME counts deaths assigned to malaria as the underlying cause. The dashed line is equality, where the PfPR-ACM estimate for the age group alone would equal UN IGME's under-five malaria deaths; the y-axis differs between panels. Countries are coloured by UN M49 sub-region (Middle Africa as Central Africa). Across the 42 countries the PfPR-ACM model attributes 39,000 deaths (95% uncertainty interval -1,000 to 77,000) under 1 month, 159,000 (106,000 to 210,000) at 1–11 months and 429,000 (354,000 to 501,000) at 1–4 years, against 440,000 UN IGME malaria deaths. Spearman correlation with UN IGME malaria deaths: 0.85 (A), 0.88 (B), 0.92 (C); the PfPR-ACM estimate for the age group alone is higher than UN IGME's under-five malaria deaths in 2 (A), 4 (B), 20 (C) of 42 countries. Vertical lines are 95% uncertainty intervals of the PfPR-ACM estimates, from 500 joint draws of MAP's national PfPR[2–10], the all-cause deaths and the PfPR–death curves (Supplementary Methods); horizontal lines are the 90% bounds of the UN IGME (CA-CODE) malaria deaths. Intervals that go below zero are drawn to zero (38 (A), 0 (B), 0 (C) countries). Intervals beyond the y-axis are truncated with an arrow: South Sudan in A (upper bound 2.3); South Sudan in B (upper bound 5.9); South Sudan in C (upper bound 9.2). CA-CODE takes malaria from WHO estimates in 13 low-burden countries.
