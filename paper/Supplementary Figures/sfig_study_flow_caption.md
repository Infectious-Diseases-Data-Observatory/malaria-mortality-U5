# Figure caption — primary sample inclusion (GADM admin-1 units)

**Figure. Sample inclusion for the primary analysis.** Of 204 DHS, MIS and MICS surveys conducted since 2000 in the 40 malaria endemic countries of mainland sub-Saharan Africa and Madagascar (113 DHS, 5 MIS, 86 MICS), 161 recorded complete birth histories (113 DHS, 5 MIS, 43 MICS; 38 countries); the other 43 MICS surveys recorded only summary birth histories. 25 surveys (14 DHS, 5 MIS, 6 MICS) were excluded because at least one required covariate was unavailable in every survey × unit area, and 136 surveys (99 DHS, 37 MICS) from 37 countries were included. Not counted in the diagram are 13 surveys (10 with complete birth histories) from Lesotho, which is malaria free and has no Malaria Atlas Project prevalence surface, and from Comoros and São Tomé and Príncipe, which lie outside the GADM admin-1 units.

In the included surveys, 9,241,771 child age-band records were entered within the 60 months before interview; counts from here are records, not unique children. 1,242,296 were excluded because the band could not be completed by the interview (1,167,873) or began outside 2000–2024 (74,423); 168,555 because the record's area could not be placed in a GADM admin-1 unit (66,826 in survey-regions not matched to a boundary; 36,290 in Zanzibar, which has no unit) or lay in malaria-free or island units or in units more than half malaria free (65,439); and 28,793 because at least one required covariate was unavailable (first missing: health expenditure 28,793). Each record is placed in the GADM 4.1 admin-1 units (KEMRI-Wellcome merge; national boundaries for Botswana, Ghana, Kenya, Madagascar, Malawi, mainland Tanzania and Uganda) through its smallest reported administrative area: in the unit holding at least 90% of that area's population, or else in the union of the units each holding at least 5%. The primary sample has 7,802,127 records and 108,747 deaths from 2,347,261 children of 1,127,215 mothers (complete birth histories), in 1,634 survey × unit areas.

Record counts over all 166 processed surveys, including the excluded ones, are in the full ledger: 10,657,524 band entries, 8,891,429 eligible records, 8,624,561 placed in 1,910 survey × unit areas.

Seven completed-month bands are analysed separately: <1, 1–5, 6–11, 12–23, 24–35, 36–47 and 48–59. The outcome is death during the band. Annual MAP PfPR₂–₁₀ of the record's unit (population-weighted over its admin-1 units) and the national covariates are assigned at band-entry year. The 17 adjustment variables comprise survey × unit summaries of maternal age at first birth, education, wealth, urban residence, DTP3/measles coverage, facility delivery, short birth interval, improved water, improved sanitation, electricity, wasting and stunting, computed from the DHS and MICS recodes, plus national annual log child HIV incidence, log GDP per capita, log health expenditure per capita and political stability. For South Sudan (independent from July 2011), GDP per capita, health expenditure per capita and political stability for band-entry years before 2011 are Sudan's values for the same year (2005–2010: 57,509 records, 955 deaths); its child HIV incidence is its own series.
Counts are read from the saved build, eligibility, placement, unit and selection ledgers and reconciled with the seven age-band fits. See [counts](flow_counts.csv) and [survey selection](survey_selection.csv).
