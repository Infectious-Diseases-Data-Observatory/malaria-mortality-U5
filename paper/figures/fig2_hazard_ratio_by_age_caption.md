# Figure 2 caption — hazard ratio of death by age band, relative to zero prevalence

**Figure. Malaria prevalence and age-specific all-cause mortality.** Hazard ratio of death in each age band at national PfPR[2–10] relative to zero prevalence, from the seven age-band models of the PfPR-ACM model. Lines are point estimates and shaded areas pointwise 95% confidence intervals (conditional on the estimated smoothing parameters). Curves are solid over the central 95% of each band's exposure distribution and dashed from zero to its 2.5th percentile (about 2%), where the zero-prevalence reference is reached by extrapolation. The attributable fraction at prevalence P is 1 − 1/HR(P). For example, at 40% prevalence the hazard ratio is 1.08 (1.01–1.14) for neonates and 2.30 (2.00–2.65) at 24–35 months.
