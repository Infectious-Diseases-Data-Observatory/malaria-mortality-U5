# Supplementary figure caption: country fixed effects

Sensitivity of the fitted PfPR-mortality relationship to replacing the country random intercept with a fixed effect for each country, so that the effect of PfPR[2-10] is estimated from variation within countries only. Log mortality hazard ratios relative to PfPR[2-10] = 20% by completed-month age band, with pointwise 95% conditional intervals shaded, from the PfPR-ACM model with country random intercepts (the primary analysis) and from a refit of the same seven models on the identical sample with country fixed effects. Sample, exposure, covariates, reference knots, basis dimensions, gamma = 2, offset and the survey and survey-unit random intercepts are unchanged. Each curve is drawn over the central 95% of its own exposure distribution. At 1 to 4 years the hazard ratio at a PfPR[2-10] of 40%, as compared with no transmission, was 1.52 to 2.19 with country fixed effects (primary 1.75 to 2.30). Applied to 2024, the refitted curves give 534,000 malaria-attributable deaths with UN IGME all-cause mortality (primary 583,000; +21% relative to the UN IGME (CA-CODE) estimate of malaria deaths) and 495,000 with IHME (primary 551,000; +16% relative to IHME). The two models are fitted to the same records, so the comparison is descriptive.
