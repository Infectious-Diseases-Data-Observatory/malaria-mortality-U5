# Supplementary figure caption: country fixed effects

Sensitivity of the fitted PfPR-mortality relationship to replacing the country random intercept with a fixed effect for each country, so that the effect of PfPR[2-10] is estimated from variation within countries only. Mortality hazard ratios relative to zero prevalence (PfPR[2-10] = 0) by completed-month age band, with pointwise 95% conditional intervals shaded, from the PfPR-ACM model with country random intercepts (the primary analysis) and from a refit of the same seven models on the identical sample with country fixed effects. Sample, exposure, covariates, reference knots, basis dimensions, gamma = 2, offset and the survey and survey-unit random intercepts are unchanged. Each curve is solid over the central 95% of its own exposure distribution and dashed from zero to its 2.5th percentile, where the reference is reached by extrapolation into sparse data; the primary curves are those of Figure 2. At 1 to 4 years the hazard ratio at a PfPR[2-10] of 40%, as compared with no transmission, was 1.78 to 2.38 with country fixed effects (primary 1.88 to 2.44). Applied to 2024, the refitted curves give 615,000 malaria-attributable deaths with UN IGME all-cause mortality (primary 627,000; +40% relative to the UN IGME (CA-CODE) estimate of malaria deaths) and 582,000 with IHME (primary 596,000; +36% relative to IHME). The two models are fitted to the same records, so the comparison is descriptive.
