# Supplementary figure: malaria's share of under-five deaths against PfPR and between sources, 2024

Malaria's share of under-five deaths in 2024 against national population-weighted MAP *Pf*PR~2–10~ for 42 sub-Saharan African countries. The share for the *Pf*PR–all-cause mortality (PfPR-ACM) model is attributable: the share of IHME all-cause under-five deaths that would not occur at *Pf*PR 0, from the seven age-band hazard ratios, so it includes deaths caused indirectly by malaria. The IHME and UN IGME shares count deaths assigned to malaria as the cause. In panels A and B each country appears at its *Pf*PR; grey lines join its PfPR-ACM model value (teal circles) to (A) IHME (navy triangles: IHME under-five malaria deaths divided by IHME all-cause under-five deaths) or (B) UN IGME (orange diamonds: the CA-CODE 2026 published malaria share of under-five deaths, with its published uncertainty bounds, interval level not stated in the download). Comparator points are shifted slightly right; darker lines mark the 7 countries with the largest absolute difference from the PfPR-ACM model value for each source, which are labelled in both of that source's panels. Panels C and D plot the PfPR-ACM model share against the IHME and UN IGME shares, one point per country, with the line of equality (dashed); vertical bars are the model's 95% intervals and horizontal bars in D the CA-CODE bounds. The model share is higher than IHME's in 36 of 42 countries and higher than UN IGME's in 39 (Spearman correlation between the model and IHME shares 0.84, UN IGME 0.80). Model intervals (95%) reflect only the age-band hazard-ratio uncertainty, drawn independently across bands, with *Pf*PR and IHME all-cause deaths held fixed, so they understate total uncertainty; IHME uncertainty intervals are not shown. The CA-CODE share is computed on CA-CODE's own total of cause-specific deaths, which can differ from the UN IGME all-cause count (DR Congo: 428,472 against 385,039 deaths). Hollow diamonds mark countries flagged in CA-CODE's published input file for WHO malaria estimates rather than its verbal-autopsy model (Madagascar, Djibouti, Ethiopia, Zimbabwe, Somalia, Eswatini, Comoros, Eritrea, Botswana). National *Pf*PR is the population-weighted mean over the area covered by the 2024 MAP surface (fixed 2020 population weights); coverage is below 95% of the population in 11 countries (Eswatini 3%, Botswana 50%, Namibia 61%, Rwanda 81%, Kenya 82%, Ethiopia 83%, Mauritania 84%, Djibouti 90%, Burundi 90%, Eritrea 94%, Chad 94%). Across countries the PfPR-ACM model share rises with *Pf*PR almost by construction; Spearman correlation with *Pf*PR is 0.86 for IHME and 0.75 for UN IGME (0.63 excluding the WHO-estimate countries). Unweighted median share across the 42 countries: PfPR-ACM model 21.5%, IHME 13.2%, UN IGME 8.2%. Cabo Verde, Lesotho and São Tomé and Príncipe have no MAP estimate, and Sudan and South Africa are not in the IHME export, so all five are excluded from every panel.
