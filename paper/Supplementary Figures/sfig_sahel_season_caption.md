# Supplementary figure caption: Sahel sub-analysis with the calendar month

Sahel sub-analysis (records whose analysis unit has its centroid at or north of 10°N) with the calendar month at risk added. (A) Mortality hazard ratios relative to zero prevalence (PfPR[2-10] = 0) at <1, 1-5 and 6-11 months: the Sahel refit of the subgroup analysis (all children; grey) and refits on the children whose birth month was reported (DHS b10 and MICS BH4F: month given), without (black) and with (orange, with its pointwise 95% conditional interval) a cyclic cubic spline of the calendar month at risk; the two refits give the same curves, so the black line lies under the orange one. Neonates are at risk in their birth month; at 1-5 and 6-11 months each child contributes one record per month of age, with the month of age as a factor in both refits. Curves are dashed from zero to their 2.5th percentile of PfPR[2-10]. (B) Hazard ratio by calendar month relative to the average month from the refits with month (orange line and interval) and the crude death rate in each calendar month relative to the band's average (circles). Children with a reported birth month: <1 month 421,298 children and 12,445 deaths; 1-5 months 377,357 children and 4,671 deaths; 6-11 months 364,646 children and 4,360 deaths (799 fewer deaths, 331 fewer deaths, 248 fewer deaths than in the Sahel subgroup analysis).
