# Supplementary figure caption: pfpr_splines_by_period

Sensitivity of the fitted PfPR–mortality relationship to the analysis subset. Mortality hazard ratios relative to zero prevalence (PfPR[2–10] = 0) by completed-month age band, from the PfPR-ACM model fitted to the full primary sample (black, with its pointwise 95% conditional interval shaded; the curves of Figure 2) and refitted separately in four subsets: surveys conducted up to the median survey year (2014); surveys conducted after it; child-band records with calendar year up to the median calendar year of the records (2012.7); and records after it. Each subset refit uses the same seven separate age-band models, 17 standardised covariates with the full-sample scaling, fixed HIV incidence imputation, gamma = 2 and basis dimensions as the primary analysis, with knots placed at quantiles of the subset's own predictor values. Each curve is solid over the central 95% of its own exposure distribution and dashed from zero to its 2.5th percentile, where the reference is reached by extrapolation into sparse data. Subset sizes. Surveys up to median survey year: 1,136,661 children, 3,727,882 records, 61,992 deaths, 833 survey × unit areas, 74 surveys, 34 countries; Surveys after median survey year: 1,210,600 children, 4,074,245 records, 46,755 deaths, 801 survey × unit areas, 62 surveys, 34 countries; Records up to median calendar year: 1,283,705 children, 3,934,686 records, 63,448 deaths, 1065 survey × unit areas, 95 surveys, 36 countries; Records after median calendar year: 1,290,193 children, 3,867,441 records, 45,299 deaths, 1073 survey × unit areas, 85 surveys, 35 countries. The subsets overlap the full sample and one another, so the curves are not independent and their differences are descriptive; the comparison does not constitute a test of effect modification. Subset intervals are omitted for legibility and are available in the saved curve table.
