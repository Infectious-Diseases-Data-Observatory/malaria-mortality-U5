# Supplementary figure caption — malaria-attributable mortality, IHME against UN IGME all-cause

Country PfPR-ACM malaria-attributable under-five deaths per 1,000 child-years in 2024, using IHME (x) and UN IGME (y) all-cause deaths, for the 42 burden countries; dashed line: equality. Countries below 1.5 per 1,000 in both sources (17) are not labelled. CAR, Central African Republic; DRC, Democratic Republic of the Congo; Eq. Guinea, Equatorial Guinea; RC, Republic of the Congo. Crisis-free values are used for South Sudan (a crisis year in one source). Spearman ρ = 0.96; the UN IGME / IHME ratio has median 0.85 (range 0.47–1.52).
