# Supplementary figure caption: polygons of the regional sub-analyses

GADM 4.1 admin-1 polygons in each regional sub-analysis of the subgroup refits, in the style of Figure 1. A record belongs to a sub-analysis by its source survey-region: the Sahel holds survey regions whose boundary centroid lies at or north of 12°N (dashed line) and west of 36°E, outside Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia and Djibouti; West, Central and East Africa hold the surveys of the UN M49 sub-regions of Figure 1 (Middle Africa as Central Africa). A polygon is coloured when all of its records are in the sub-analysis and in a paler tint when only some are, which happens only in the Sahel, because survey regions and polygons do not coincide; mid grey marks the other polygons of the primary analysis. (A) Sahel (≥12°N): 67 polygons; 1,551,121 child-band records and 19,603 deaths from 31 surveys in 8 countries. (B) West Africa: 173 polygons; 3,743,776 child-band records and 56,426 deaths from 61 surveys in 15 countries. (C) Central Africa: 113 polygons; 1,224,689 child-band records and 17,579 deaths from 18 surveys in 7 countries. (D) East Africa: 173 polygons; 2,735,874 child-band records and 33,671 deaths from 50 surveys in 12 countries. Southern Africa is not refitted (too few deaths and too little prevalence), so it has no panel.
