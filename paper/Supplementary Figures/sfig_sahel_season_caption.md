# Supplementary figure caption: Sahel sub-analysis with the calendar month

Sahel sub-analysis (records whose analysis unit has its centroid at or north of 10°N and west of 36°E, excluding the Horn of Africa) with the calendar month at risk added. (A) Mortality hazard ratios relative to zero prevalence (PfPR[2-10] = 0) at <1, 1-5 and 6-11 months: the band model fitted to all the sub-analysis's children (grey) and refitted to the children whose birth month was reported (DHS b10 and MICS BH4F: month given), without (black) and with (orange, with its pointwise 95% conditional interval) a cyclic cubic spline of the calendar month at risk; where the two refits give the same curve, the black line lies under the orange one. Neonates are at risk in their birth month; at 1-5 and 6-11 months each child contributes one record per month of age, with the month of age as a factor in both refits, so that a death's record carries its calendar month of death. Curves are dashed from zero to their 2.5th percentile of PfPR[2-10]. (B) Hazard ratio by calendar month relative to the average month from the refits with month (orange line and interval) and the crude death rate in each calendar month relative to the band's average (circles). All children: 57 surveys in 14 countries. Children with a reported birth month: <1 month 421,298 children and 12,445 deaths (of 13,244); 1-5 months 377,357 children and 4,671 deaths (of 5,002); 6-11 months 364,646 children and 4,360 deaths (of 4,608).
