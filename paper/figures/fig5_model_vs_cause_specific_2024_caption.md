# Figure 5 caption — PfPR-ACM against CA-CODE malaria mortality, by country

**Figure. Malaria-attributable under-five mortality in 2024: PfPR-ACM model against CA-CODE malaria deaths.** Each point is a country; rates are deaths per 1,000 child-years under 5. PfPR-ACM estimates using UN IGME all-cause mortality (y) against CA-CODE malaria deaths (x), both over the UN WPP population under 5. The PfPR-ACM estimate counts all deaths averted if prevalence fell to zero (direct and indirect); CA-CODE counts deaths assigned to malaria as the underlying cause. The dashed line is equality. Point area is proportional to the population under 5 (UN WPP). Countries with an estimate of at least 4 per 1,000 from either source are named (11 of 42). Spearman correlation: 0.92; the PfPR-ACM estimate is higher in 31 of 42 countries. Vertical lines are 95% uncertainty intervals of the PfPR-ACM estimates, from 500 joint draws of MAP's national PfPR[2–10], the all-cause deaths and the PfPR–death curves (Supplementary Methods); horizontal lines are the 90% bounds of the CA-CODE malaria deaths. The lower bound exceeds the CA-CODE estimate in 19 countries, and the upper bound is below it in 3. Intervals beyond the axes are truncated with an arrow: South Sudan (upper bound 16.4). CA-CODE assigns malaria deaths to ages 1–59 months as a whole and none under 1 month, and takes malaria from WHO estimates in 13 low-burden countries.
