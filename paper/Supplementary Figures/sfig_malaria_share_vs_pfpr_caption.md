# Supplementary figure: malaria's share of under-five deaths against PfPR and between sources, 2024

Malaria's share of under-five deaths in 2024 in 42 sub-Saharan African countries: the malaria-attributable share from the PfPR-ACM model compared with the share of deaths assigned to malaria by IHME and by CA-CODE. The model's share is the share of all-cause under-five deaths that would not occur at *Pf*PR 0, so it includes deaths caused indirectly by malaria; it is based on IHME all-cause deaths in panels A and C and on UN IGME all-cause deaths in panels B and D. A, B: each country is plotted at its national *Pf*PR~2–10~ (Malaria Atlas Project); a grey line joins its model value (teal circles) to the IHME value (A, navy triangles: IHME under-five malaria deaths divided by IHME all-cause under-five deaths) or the CA-CODE value (B, orange diamonds: CA-CODE's published malaria share of under-five deaths). Comparator points are shifted slightly to the right. C, D: the model's share against the IHME share (C) and the CA-CODE share (D), one point per country; the dashed line shows equality. Vertical bars are the model's 95% uncertainty intervals; orange bars are CA-CODE's published bounds (interval level not stated in the download); IHME intervals are not shown. For each source the 7 countries with the largest absolute difference from the model are named, with darker joining lines in A and B. Hollow diamonds mark countries flagged in CA-CODE's published input file for WHO malaria estimates in place of its verbal-autopsy model (Madagascar, Ethiopia, Comoros, Somalia, Djibouti, Eritrea, Zimbabwe, Eswatini, Botswana). The model's share is higher than IHME's in 30 of 42 countries and higher than CA-CODE's in 31 (Spearman correlation 0.84 and 0.81). Median share across the 42 countries: model 21.0% (IHME-based) and 21.2% (UN IGME-based), IHME 13.2%, CA-CODE 8.2%. Spearman correlation with *Pf*PR: IHME 0.83, CA-CODE 0.77 (0.67 without the WHO-estimate countries). In a crisis year the model share is the crisis-free value (South Sudan, IHME-based). The CA-CODE share is computed on CA-CODE's own total of cause-specific deaths, which can differ from the UN IGME all-cause count (DR Congo: 428,472 against 385,039 deaths). Lesotho has no MAP national estimate, Cabo Verde and São Tomé and Príncipe are outside the 42-country burden set, and Sudan and South Africa are not in the IHME export, so all five are excluded from every panel.
